Meenakshi Temple, Madurai

The Meenakshi Temple in Madurai, Tamil Nadu, is one of the most prominent and historic temples in South India. Dedicated to Goddess Meenakshi (a form of Parvati) and her consort Sundareswarar (Shiva), the temple is renowned for its stunning Dravidian architecture and towering gopurams (gateways). The temple complex is a vibrant hub of devotion, art, and cultural history.
